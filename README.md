# Sudo-not-Sumo
Documenting my path through DFIR/AI Forensics.
Folders:
- CTFs: CTF(Capture The Flag) Writeups. Further separated into Blue Team and AI CTF Writeups. Aiming for updates every other Saturday.
- OvertheWire&THM: My OvertheWire Wargames notes and solutions (Bandit, Leviathan, Natas), along with notes and takeaways from various TryHackMe Rooms.
- Big Potato Stuff: Braindump / Thought Exercise on different topics of my interest, involving cybersecurity or AI.
- AI Interrogation: Reports on experimental conversations with different models. Probing for specific characteristics/behaviors or defense mechanisms. Updated every other Saturday.

My name is Yunjae Park, and I am a class of 2030 at the University of Texas at Austin, Majoring in Computer Science. 
This all started with just a few weeks left before my freshman year, when I suddenly grew very curious about the field of cybersecurity, specifically in becoming a sort of cyber detective for AI.
As I write this, about a month into my first semester at UTCS, that spark has grown into a fierce passion with concrete actions, specifically now in the growing field of AI safety/forensics.
I will continue to record my progress here.
